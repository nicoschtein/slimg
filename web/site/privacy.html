Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Slimg works entirely on your Mac. It doesn't collect anything about you or your images, and it never connects to the internet. This policy covers the Slimg app, the slimg command-line tool and this website.

On your Mac

Slimg reads the images you give it and writes the smaller version back to the same file. With Keep originals on, it also writes a copy of the original next to it, named like name.bak.jpg. Those files are the only things Slimg writes.

Slimg doesn't save your settings or a list of the files you opened. Each time you open it, it starts with the defaults.

What Slimg doesn't do

  • No network connections. Slimg works the same with Wi-Fi off.
  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, ads or tracking.
  • No third-party code that talks to anyone. The compression libraries built into Slimg work on the image and nothing else.
  • No automatic updates. Slimg doesn't check for new versions.

iCloud, background work and notifications

Slimg doesn't use iCloud, doesn't run in the background and doesn't send notifications. When you quit it, nothing of Slimg keeps running.

Purchases

Slimg is free. There is nothing to buy and no payment information is ever asked for.

Backups

Your images, and any .bak originals, are backed up the same way as the rest of your files, for example by Time Machine. Slimg adds nothing of its own to your backups.

Deleting

To remove Slimg, drag it from Applications to the Trash. For the command-line tool, delete the slimg file you installed. Slimg leaves no data behind.

Children

Slimg collects nothing from anyone, children included.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It's hosted by Cloudflare, which handles each request to deliver the page and to stop abuse. The downloads are served from GitHub, which hosts the Slimg releases.

If you write to us with the contact form, your message is sent to our inbox by email so we can reply. We use it only to answer you and don't add you to any list.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
